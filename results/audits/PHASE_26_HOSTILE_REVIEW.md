# PHASE 26 — Hostile reviewer report (self-review, reviewer-perspective)

Simulated referee report; verdicts on the draft as it stands.

## Major objections

1. **Selection into architecture is not addressed, only acknowledged.**
   Donors allocate TC to weak-governance recipients; β_TC < 0 could be pure
   sorting on (declining) state capacity. Within-year shuffle placebo shows
   the estimate is not mechanical, and leads are null, but neither rules out
   time-varying selection on recipient quality. *Mitigation in draft:
   explicit Level-B ceiling; flagged as core limitation. Still vulnerable.*

2. **Compositional interpretation ambiguity.** Share coefficients are
   relative to "debt relief + other", a mixture that itself bundles
   humanitarian/admin flows. A referee will ask which margin drives β_TC.
   *Partially addressed: exclusion-check in falsification suite drops
   non-projective flows; headline wording stays compositional.*

3. **Tying data quality.** Post-2005 tying flags are increasingly
   non-reported; share_untied is therefore selected. *Draft restricts tying
   analysis to reported subsample; reviewer will still press.*

4. **usd_irtc vs aid_t D-codes divergence** (TC intensity vs TC projects) —
   requires reconciliation or explicit choice. *Flagged in audit; aid_t
   version used for shares.*

5. **Growth regression literature fatigue**: aid→growth conditional
   regressions are a known minefield (Roodman critique applies). The paper
   must foreground the descriptive-decomposition contribution rather than
   the outcome coefficients. *Recommendation adopted: lead with donor
   decomposition + falsification suite; tone down β headlines.*

6. **Multiple testing across 320 coefficients.** Frozen estimands mitigate
   but a referee will ask for a stated multiplicity policy. *All estimates
   reported; no star-mining; table is exhaustive, not selected.*

7. **Procurement section is thin** (partial sample). *Labeled exploratory;
   consider moving to appendix.*

## Minor
- Only 6 references cited in text; literature table has 50 verified entries —
  manuscript should cite the policy and composition literature more fully
  before submission.
- Figures must be separate files for submission (per journal rules) — done.
- Ensure every figure/table is cited in text — currently loosely satisfied.

## Verdict
Publishable as a descriptive/decomposition paper at World Development **if**
framing stays Level-B, the decomposition is the headline, and references are
expanded. Not viable at JDE without an identification upgrade.

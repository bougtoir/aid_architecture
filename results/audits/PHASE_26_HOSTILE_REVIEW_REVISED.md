# FINAL HOSTILE REVIEW — 4 personas (post-revision)

## 1. Development economist
- *Residual complaint:* headline β_TC is imprecise (p≈0.25); the paper now
  says so, but the abstract still leads with the association. Acceptable as
  "adjusted association" given the permutation evidence, but a referee may
  demand the word "weak" or confidence-interval framing. Defensible.
- *Strength acknowledged:* incremental-R² decomposition is now honest —
  donor identity matters but is not unilateral; project-share caveat is a
  genuinely useful nuance.
- *Verdict:* publishable as a measurement/decomposition paper with careful
  framing; will still press on selection.

## 2. Causal-inference reviewer
- Lead placebo null + shuffle extremeness ~2% + pseudo-outcome null is a
  decent falsification suite, but none of these identify causal effects:
  time-varying recipient need is the standing threat and is now confronted
  prominently. Claim ceiling B is respected everywhere.
- The share-contrast framing (reallocation vs omitted composition) is
  correctly stated; no residual "TC harms" language remains.

## 3. OECD/CRS specialist
- Modality-D TC ≈2% of disbursements is now disclosed — good; a CRS expert
  would otherwise accuse the paper of mis-measuring TC. Recommendation
  flagged: results should be read as "modality-D TC share", since most TC is
  embedded in projects (usd_irtc measure reported separately).
- Tying non-reporting bias disclosed; procurement section correctly
  exploratory; supplier-country ≠ domestic VA stated.
- Donor codelist defect that blanked Figure 2 was real and is fixed.

## 4. Journal editor (e.g., World Development)
- Fit: architecture/decomposition contribution suits WD; the aid–growth
  association results alone would not clear the bar — the paper now leads
  with measurement + decomposition, which is the right positioning.
- Remaining weaknesses: no quarter-matched seasonal/dynamics, procurement
  sample is thin; figures are submission-quality not journal-styled.
- Verdict: defensible submission package; no blocking integrity issues.

## Summary verdicts
- Blocking issues found in revision: unit error (fixed), blank Fig 2 (fixed,
  root-caused), over-claimed donor decomposition (fixed), reference DOIs

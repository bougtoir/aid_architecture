# WORLD_DEVELOPMENT_FIT_AUDIT

Source: ScienceDirect Guide for Authors, World Development (accessed 2026-09-25).

## Journal fit
- WD is a multidisciplinary development-studies monthly; aid effectiveness,
  aid allocation, and development-finance papers are core territory. An
  integrative measurement + decomposition paper on aid architecture fits
  better than a marginal aid–growth regression — the revision positions the
  manuscript accordingly (measurement/decomposition primary, outcome
  associations secondary, TC result illustrative).
- Empirical defensibility: panel FE + falsification suite + declared
  non-causal ceiling is appropriate for WD; the paper must foreground
  architecture measurement and donor heterogeneity.

## Submission mechanics (official guidance)
- Word file (editable), double-spaced Abstract/Text/References; min 4,500 /
  max ~10,000 words excl. references, figures, tables.
- Abstract within manuscript file, max 300 words (stricter portal notes
  suggest ≤250; we target ~190–220). No citations/abbreviation-dependent
  text.
- 3–6 keywords.
- Highlights: 3–5, each ≤125 characters (without spaces), separate file.
- Double-anonymized: no author names in manuscript file or filename;
  separate title page; blinded + unblinded copies prepared.
- Data availability statement required at submission.
- Figures/tables as separate files at submission; supplementary material
  supported; no strict low reference cap — focused bibliography fine.

## Decisions
- Title tightened to: "Beyond Aid Volume: Donor Heterogeneity, Aid
  Architecture, and Medium-Run Development Outcomes" (drops unmeasured
  "Local Economic Participation"/"Capacity Formation" claims).
- Figure hierarchy: Fig 1 architecture trends, Fig 2 donor heatmap
  (central), Fig 3 TC-window coefficients; ODA/GDP histogram → supplement.
- Tables: 4 compact main tables; full 320-coefficient grid in supplement.
- Dashboard described as supplementary model-based scenario explorer only.
